Why this matters at work

Farhan in accounts needs a payment-reminder letter. He pastes the customer ageing report — 340 names, phone numbers and outstanding amounts — into a free AI site. The letter that comes back is excellent. The report is now on a server we do not control. You will know in two seconds whether something is safe to paste — and exactly what to type instead.

Workplace example

Why one paste is hard to undo

People expect a paste to behave like a draft email they can unsend. It does not.

Stage recap — 7. Security & Responsible Use

What to carry forward

What I should remember

  • Explain why reuse matters more than complexity; explain what a second factor is, without jargon
  • Name the four pressure signals that appear in almost every attack
  • Say what counts as personal data, including the surprising cases; sort information into four classes in a few seconds
  • Tell an approved tool from an unapproved one at a glance
  • Name the five kinds of information that must never go into an AI tool

What I should do

  • THREE OR FOUR WORDS — "copper-lantern-quiet-mango"
  • URGENCY — today, before close, immediately
  • COLLECT LESS — if you do not need a field, do not ask for it
  • You signed in with a personal email, not your work one

What I should avoid

  • Adding a number at the end
  • Changing one letter to a symbol
