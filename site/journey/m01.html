Why this matters at work

Ramesh runs dispatch at the Bhiwandi warehouse. Every Friday he writes delay notes for the shipments that missed their date. He already knows every order number, every reason, every new date. What takes him two hours is finding the words. You hand over the facts. A clean draft comes back in a minute. Your time goes into checking it, not typing it.
