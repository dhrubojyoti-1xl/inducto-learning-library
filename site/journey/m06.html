Why this matters at work

A weekly production meeting in Pune runs for an hour. Everyone leaves clear about the decisions. Nine days later, two of the four actions have not started, and each person believed somebody else owned them. Three columns — decision, owner, date — sent within the hour.

Workplace example

Why minutes fail

Most meeting notes record the conversation. Almost nobody needs the conversation.

Try it — copy-paste prompt
Copy this prompt
Below are my rough notes from a meeting. Produce three sections and nothing else. DECISIONS: one line each. ACTIONS: one line each, with the owner's name and a date. OPEN: anything not settled. Use only my notes. Where no owner or date was given, write [OWNER?] or [DATE?].
