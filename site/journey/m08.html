Why this matters at work

Nikhil coordinates projects for a Hyderabad office. He asks for "a project plan for a warehouse fit-out". He gets a polished twelve-week plan, with roles the company does not have, for a country it does not operate in. Four lines of context, and the plan describes your project. In Role-Based Prompts: Meera runs quality checks at a Coimbatore plant. She asks for an explanation of a rejection trend to send to the sales team. What comes back is full of process capability indices. Sales read two lines and call her instead.

Workplace example

Four lines it cannot guess

Without these it invents a company, a reader and a situation. All three will be wrong.

Write a reply email to a client in Sharjah. Context: this is the third delay this quarter, our contact there is new to the account, and we cannot offer a discount. Instructions: under 120 words, apologetic without accepting liability, end with a question rather than a statement.
