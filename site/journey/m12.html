Why this matters at work

Sanjay runs stores at a Nashik unit. Six years ago he used one password for a shopping site. He uses the same one at work. The shopping site was breached in 2021. Nobody told him, and the password still works here. Different passwords everywhere, remembered by a manager, not by you. In Multi-Factor Authentication: Ayesha handles HR records in Dubai. Her password leaked in a breach at a site she had forgotten about. Someone used it at 2am. They got the password right and still could not get in, because a code went to her phone.
