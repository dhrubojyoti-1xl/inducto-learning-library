Why this matters at work

Kavita answers support tickets for a Bengaluru team. A customer disputes a charge. She asks an AI tool what the policy says, and it quotes clause 7.3. She pastes the clause into her reply. There is no clause 7.3. Four checks, under a minute, and this never leaves your desk.
