Why this matters at work

Harish runs projects in Abu Dhabi. A client asks, during an audit, whether any of their drawings have been processed by AI tools. He genuinely does not know. Nor does anyone else, because nobody kept a record. Approved tools, the right settings, and a habit of noting what you used.

Workplace example

Approval is more than being logged in

The same model can be safe or unsafe depending on the account you signed in with — but the account alone does not make a use approved. The tool, the configuration and what you are using it for all have to be approved too.
