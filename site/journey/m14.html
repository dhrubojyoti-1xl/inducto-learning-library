Why this matters at work

Manoj handles sales admin in Kochi. He wants to finish a report at home, so he forwards the customer contact list to his personal email. Nothing bad happens. It is still a reportable transfer of personal data out of the company. Four rules that cover almost every decision you will face. In Handling Confidential Information: Divya supports the legal team in Bengaluru. She reuses an old slide in a supplier presentation. It still carries the discount structure agreed with a different supplier. Nobody notices in the room. The photograph circulates.
