Why this matters at work

Deepa prepares the MIS pack for a Chennai office. Every Monday she pastes the same 40 rows into a document, rewrites the headings, and turns the numbers into sentences for the management summary. The analysis takes twenty minutes. The formatting takes three hours. The shape work is exactly what these tools do best. In AI Limitations: Rajesh handles purchase for an Ahmedabad unit. He asks an AI tool for the current price of a steel grade. It answers with a figure per tonne. He builds a quotation around it. The real rate is 19 per cent higher.
